Roadmap

What works today, and what is planned

Mirra is under active development. This page lists what is planned and roughly in what order — so you can see where it is heading and whether it is heading somewhere useful to you.

Numbers are an order, not a schedule. Timing may change, and some features may never arrive at all if nobody turns out to want them.

Where things stand

  • 1.0 — Client records Done. A searchable list, client cards, calling and messaging, socials and messengers.
  • 1.1 — Staying signed in Done. The session resumes on its own instead of asking every time.
  • 1.2 — Tags and birthdays Done. Tags written inside notes, birthdays with ages.
  • 1.3 — Birthday reminders Done. A note on the card and a marker in the list on the day.
  • 2.0 — Linked clients Done. A parent to a child, one partner to another, a guardian to the person they care for.
  • Next — Appointments Booking clients into a calendar, with a view of the week ahead.
  • Later — Income What was earned, from whom and when, without a separate spreadsheet.
  • Later — Routes For those who travel to clients: where and in what order.
  • Later — Message templates Reminders and confirmations that do not have to be typed out each time.
  • Later — Analytics Who comes back, who has not been seen in a while, when the busy weeks are.

An honest caveat

Mirra stores none of your data. It works accountless: no accounts, no user database, and no place in the middle between you and Google. The site serves your browser the app's code — everything else goes straight from your browser into your own account.

That is done for safety: what does not exist cannot be breached or lost. But it has a consequence worth stating plainly: what Mirra can do, and how reliably, depends on Google's infrastructure. If Google changes the rules for a service, Mirra changes with them.

Each new feature will ask for the narrowest permission that does the job, separately, and you can withdraw any of them at any time. Details are in the privacy policy.

Suggestions

Mirra grows with the people using it. If something you need is missing, or something here looks wrong, say so — that is how this list gets its order.
