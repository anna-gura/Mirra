What Mirra is

A workspace for keeping client records, running in your browser and working with spreadsheets in your own Google account.

Mirra stores none of your data and is not an intermediary between you and Google. The server you open the app from serves only its code; everything else happens in your browser.

Because of that, what Mirra can do and how reliably depends on Google's infrastructure: if Google changes its interfaces or restricts access, some features may stop working, and that is outside our control.

We also do not guarantee that the site itself will always be available. Since the code is open, you can always run Mirra yourself — instructions on GitHub.
